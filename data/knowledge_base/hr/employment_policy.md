# Employee Records and HR Operations Policy (HR-101)

- Employee records are confidential and should be accessed only for legitimate HR or operational purposes.
- The employee database is the source of truth for employee status, department, manager, employment type, and current leave balances.
- Policy documents define eligibility and rules; MCP employee tools provide live employee-specific data.
- Before creating a leave request, identify the employee, retrieve the applicable policy, and check the employee's current leave balance.
- Approving or rejecting leave is a write operation and must be explicitly requested by an authorized user.
- Do not infer or invent employee IDs, balances, or approval status.
