# Employee Leave Policy (HR-102)

## Eligibility

- Full-time and contract employees marked as active may submit leave requests.
- Leave is subject to available balance and manager approval.
- Employees should submit planned annual leave at least 7 calendar days before the start date where practical.

## Annual Leave

- Full-time employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave per calendar year, accrued monthly.
- Contract employees receive leave according to their contract; the operational database stores the applicable balance.
- Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may be carried into Q1 of the next calendar year.
- A request cannot be approved when the requested days exceed the current annual-leave balance.

## Sick Leave

- Employees receive up to 12 sick-leave days per calendar year according to their recorded balance.
- A doctor's note is required for more than four consecutive sick-leave days.
- A request cannot be approved when the requested days exceed the current sick-leave balance.

## Parental Leave

- Primary caregiver: 26 weeks.
- Secondary caregiver: 8 weeks.
- Parental-leave requests require HR review and are not deducted through the annual/sick leave workflow.

## Approval and Conflicts

- New annual and sick leave requests begin in `pending` status.
- Only a pending request may be approved, rejected, or cancelled.
- Overlapping pending or approved requests for the same employee are not allowed.
- Leave balance is deducted only when a request is approved.
